# Kumitachi (7)

1. Four basic attacks, establishing initial distance, timing, and initial parry-counter concepts.
2. Tsuki (thrust) parry and counter sequences.
3. Continuous tsuki pressure drills.
4. Changing hanmi and counter-attacking from underneath.
5. Advanced blending and distance management.
6. Dynamic timing and cutting applications.
7. Final paired sword form integrating fluid spatial control and aiki principles.
