# Sword Waza/Exercises

## Suriage Men 摺上げ面
- Suriage - Sliding parry
- An upwards motion of the sword that does a sliding deflection on the opponents sword.
- use hips, tsuka slightly out, not taking kissaki offline
- perform while moving in, to peform menuchi afterwards
- [Kanai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qpx6dIaLhlc&t=82s)
- [Chiba - Davinder Bath](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esZDGv3QFno&t=355s)

## Kaeshi Suriage Men 返し摺上げ面
- Sliding parry reversal
- Countering a suriage with another suriage.
- Shitachi barely moves for the counter, uchitachi is moving in with their own suriage.
- [Kanai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qpx6dIaLhlc&t=122s)

## Kiriotoshi Tsuki 切落し突き
- Kiriotoshi - dropping cut
- strike their sword from kiriotoshi, then tsuki
- hip turns as strike hits.
- can be done stepping back raise/cut as uchitachi does a shōmen-uchi stepping in
   - Response: ukenagashi, ayumi ashi, yokomen-uchi/shōmen-uchi to head
- [Kanai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qpx6dIaLhlc&t=194s)

## Suriage Kote 摺上げ小手
- like suriage men, but sidestepping ayumi ashi as we move in.
- sword travels up, ends up loaded on right shoulder from the sidestep
- readjust above head, strike kote
- [Kanai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qpx6dIaLhlc&t=336s)

## Makiotoshi Men 巻落し面
- Makiotoshi - twist down
- sword touch, use connection to flip down/out/completely across into all the way behind and shōmen cut
- flip is tip rotational
- [Kanai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qpx6dIaLhlc&t=356s)

## Ukenagashi 受流し
- Flowing reception
- There are a lot of different versions of this, but it's generally receiving the attack in a way that moves the attach offline, but responds in your own attack
- [Paul Frank - 3 Aikido styles](https://youtu.be/0tHwQd6oc6s?si=WdsCoN8BN0Q-be1J&t=198)
  - Non-aikido source, but sums up the styles I've seen in Aikido
- I find the discussion on that video interesting when he talks about Koryu (e.g. pre -Do arts)
  - [Paul Frank - Koryu (Katori Shinto Ryu)](https://youtu.be/0tHwQd6oc6s?si=WdsCoN8BN0Q-be1J&t=360)
  - Note that version of ukenagashi seems like.. Suriage!
- [Kanai responding to kiriotoshi with ukenagashi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qpx6dIaLhlc&t=276s)
- [Chiba (Nagashi Uchi) - Davinder Bath](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esZDGv3QFno&t=40s)

## Suri Otoshi 摺落し
- Sliding downwards
- Starts like Ukenagashi, but rotationally flings opponents sword down via sliding down it
- [Chiba - Davinder Bath](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esZDGv3QFno&t=148s)

## Uchi Otoshi 撃落し
- Striking downwards
- Similar to suri otoshi, but more cutting downwards than sliding
- [Chiba - Davinder Bath](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esZDGv3QFno&t=256s)

## Shikodachi Partner striking drill 四股立 (Horse Stance)
- Partner practice taking turns striking at each other in horse stance
- Theoretically could do most of the basic waza listed like this, but Chiba's versions definitely
- 四股立(ち) shiko-dachi is the wide low sumo/karate horse stance; the drill takes its name from the stance it is done in (not 仕込太刀, an earlier mis-reading)
- [Chiba - Davinder Bath](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esZDGv3QFno&t=451s)
